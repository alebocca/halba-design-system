DocsHeader opens a docs page: breadcrumb (mono uppercase, `ink-muted`, current page in `ink`), the title at 40px/44px weight 600 with tight tracking, a lead of at most two lines in `ink-muted`, and optional children such as an `Endpoint`.

`breadcrumb` is `[{label, href}]`; the last item is the current page. One `h1` per page. Sentence case; the title names the thing ("Create a payment"), not the section.
